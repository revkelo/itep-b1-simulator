iTEP Practice Simulator

A full practice run of the iTEP English exam, free and in the browser. Five sections - Grammar, Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking - each with the real countdown, plus a CEFR report from A1 to C2 when you finish.

  • Grammar - sentence completion and error detection, 10 minutes.
  • Listening - conversations and a lecture, played once.
  • Reading - two passages with comprehension questions, 20 minutes.
  • Writing - timed written responses, 25 minutes.
  • Speaking - recorded answers with preparation time.

Exam mode keeps official timing and scores at the end; study mode gives feedback after every answer. Your attempt is saved locally, so closing the tab does not lose it. No account and no download.

Independent practice tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by iTEP International.
